Snake Alert

Prototype 03

Report a snake sighting on the campus map, see reports near you, and know when an area has been marked safe.

Open the app Download for Android · APK, 62 MB

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If asked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, choose Install anyway.

Use it without installing

  1. Tap Open the app. It works in the browser on Android and iPhone.
  2. To keep it on your home screen: browser menu → Add to Home screen (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen).
